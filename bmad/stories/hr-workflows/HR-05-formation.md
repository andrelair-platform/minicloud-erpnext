## Story
**As an** employee, **I want** to request training and track it to completion **so that** development is
governed (manager + budget) and recorded.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1: Training Program / Event configured.
- [ ] AC-2: request → **manager approval** (+ budget check) → enrollment → **Training Result**.
- [ ] AC-3: notifications at request, approval and completion.

## DoD
A training request is approved, enrolled, and its result recorded against the employee.
